Media Relations

We develop brand narratives and story angles that matter, then pitch the right journalists, editors, producers, podcast hosts, and Substack writers — from local outlets to national platforms.

Brand Strategy

We help you find your voice, clarify your message, and show up consistently across every touchpoint — so your story lands the same way whether it's a press release or a podcast interview.

AI Visibility

We help clients show up in the answers — not just in the search results. As AI-generated responses reshape how people discover brands, we position you to be part of that conversation.

Influencer & Partnerships

From micro-influencers to major collaborations, we connect brands with the right voices and build partnerships that extend your reach authentically.

Events & Experiences

From launch parties and media events to large-scale consumer experiences and PR stunts — we handle both the event itself and the publicity around it, generating buzz before, during, and after.

Crisis Communications

Digital & Social Strategy

Content, community, and social storytelling that amplifies your earned media and keeps audiences engaged.

Media Training

Thought Leadership

About Spin PR

Relationship-first PR, always.

Founded in 1998, Spin PR was built on a simple belief: great PR starts with understanding what our clients need, what the media wants, and what stories are worth telling.

I lead every account personally, backed by a trusted network of specialists who bring the right expertise to every project. Together, we maintain long-term relationships with media members coast to coast — so your story gets to the right audience at the right time.

Our experience spans automotive, clean energy, luxury real estate, retail, sporting events, large-scale consumer experiences, the boating industry, and more. From one-time projects to long-term strategic campaigns, every program is built specifically for you — no one-size-fits-all.
